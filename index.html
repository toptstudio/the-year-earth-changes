“Why should I believe these hadith if they’re not in Bukhari or Muslim?”

Because Bukhari and Muslim are not the only sources of Islamic knowledge. The Prophet’s words were preserved in dozens of collections. The Sahih system is a later construction used to filter out inconvenient prophecies.

“Isn’t this just fear‑mongering?”
