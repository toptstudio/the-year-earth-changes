1. The Tawhid Equation 0.999... = 1 as the Mathematical Proof of Oneness

Before we reach the final convergence, a deeper truth must be unveiled. The very fabric of mathematics testifies to the core of Islamic belief: Tawhid, the absolute Oneness of Allah. The infinite decimal 0.999... is exactly equal to 1. This is not an approximation — it is a proven identity.

Observe the geometric series that reveals the illusion of separation:

1 – 0.99 = 0.01
0.01 – 0.0099 = 0.0001
0.0001 – 0.000099 = 0.000001
… and so forth, endlessly.

Let us define the sum of all subtracted terms S:

S = 0.99 + 0.0099 + 0.000099 + …
= 99 × (0.01 / 0.99)
= 99 × (1/99)
= 1

Thus the total amount removed from the initial 1 to reach a supposed remainder of zero is exactly 1. The infinite process exhausts itself without leaving any gap. The multiplicity of the world — represented by 0.999... — is, in the limit, identical to the One.

There is no duality. There is only the apparent interval that vanishes when one sees the sum in its entirety. The Creator and His creation are not two separate realities — the creation is nothing other than the manifestation of His command. This mathematical fact is the foundation upon which the Q‑era is built.
